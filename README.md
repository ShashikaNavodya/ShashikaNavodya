## About me
<p align="justify">
I am a Management Information Systems undergraduate (current GPA 3.71) at NSBM Green University with strong interest in software development, database systems, UI/UX designing. I have hands-on experience in C, Java, Figma, Canva, JavaScript, SQL/PSQL ,Dart  and Flutter along with knowledge in system analysis and software engineering. I have developed projects such as a Bank Management System, Hospital Management System, Library Reservation System, Agriculture App, and a game project, which improved my problem-solving and development skills.  
<br><br>
I am also active in student leadership and volunteering, serving as Treasurer of the NSBM STEMUP Club. With a strong passion for continuous learning, I aim to grow as a professional software and mobile application developer focused on building efficient and scalable solutions.
</p>
